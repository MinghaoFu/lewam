Notes & Observations

Frozen repro matches/exceeds paper. Our reproduction achieves 96.3% mean SR vs paper's 96.0%. PushT slightly exceeds (88.0% vs 84.7%).

LeWAM-GC PushT: 80.7% (promising). End-to-end finetuning achieves 80.7% vs frozen 88.0%. Some variance across seeds (66-92%). Cube & Reacher training ~12h remaining.

Scratch ablation shows encoder quality matters. Without pretrained encoder, PushT drops 14.7pp (88.0% → 73.3%), Cube drops 10.7pp (100% → 89.3%), and Reacher drops catastrophically (97.3% → 13.3%). Both best.pt and latest.pt produce 13.3% — pretrained encoder is essential for reacher.

Key checkpoint traps discovered:

  • Reacher: latest.pt outperforms best.pt (97.3% vs 73.3%)
  • TwoRoom: Must use task-specific encoder, not generic LeWM weights (100% vs 38%)
  • dm_control version must be 1.0.43 for mujoco 3.10.0 compatibility

Data on SSD-HDFS. All 4 datasets (242GB) migrated to fast SSD tier (bi_algo_a2f). Jobs symlink directly — no more 22-min decompression.
