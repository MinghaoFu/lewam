# Literature Survey — Positioning a JEPA Intuition-Guided Planner for Contact-Rich Manipulation

Survey date: 2026-06-12 (web, two agents). Knowledge cutoff Jan 2026 — items with arXiv id `25xx`/`26xx` after the cutoff are flagged **[post-cutoff, verify PDF before citing]**; their details are search-snippet-deep.

## 0. The question and the one-paragraph verdict
We are positioning a method: a JEPA latent world model carrying a fast amortized **"intuition head"** that predicts the next action **autoregressively from the latent state history `z_{≤t}` and the past actions `a_{<t}`**, trained in **embedding space** (target = action embedding), used to **warm-start CEM planning**. The survey's verdict: the bare *mechanism* ("a learned policy guides planning") is **owned** (MuZero/ExIt → TD-MPC2 → PiJEPA), and the *multi-task padded-action prior* story is **also occupied** (TD-MPC2 + Newt). The defensible contribution is **not** the mechanism. It is the conjunction, applied to **contact-rich manipulation**: (i) a prior conditioned on **past actions** (rare — recovers contact state pixels can't show), (ii) a **latent embedding-space intuition** (unclaimed for warm-starting search — helps even where the raw-action decode is at the data floor), and (iii) an **analysis** of when/why pure JEPA planning fails on contact and what the intuition restores. Target **contact-rich manipulation** (robomimic), which WM-planning papers barely report.

| **SMWM** (Sensorimotor World Models, Ivashkov/**Balestriero**/Schölkopf) | 2606.20104 (Jun'26) **[post-cutoff]** | 2D nav + 3D control from pixels | **pure planning, no policy**; latent shaped by an **inverse-dynamics regularizer** that replaces SIGReg/EMA/frozen-encoder |

**Takeaway.** JEPA *does* do continuous control, and the dominant pattern is **frozen/pretrained features + sampling-based CEM/MPC with no learned proposal**. The one that amortizes (TD-JEPA) drops search entirely (zero-shot RL). V-JEPA 2-AC's modest **grasp** numbers on a real arm are the field's own evidence that **pure CEM struggles with contact**. Non-JEPA SOTA baselines for CC: **DreamerV3** (2301.04104, actor-critic, no search), **TD-MPC2** (2310.16828), **DCWM** (2503.00653), **PWM** (2407.02466). Shared benchmarks: DMC/Meta-World/ManiSkill (RL line) vs PushT/Maze/OGBench/Cube/Reacher (JEPA-planning line); **OGBench** (2410.20092) bridges. **robomimic (lift/can/square) is essentially unreported by WM-planning papers — white space.** The one exception (deep-research 2026-06-13, verified across ~15 WM/MBRL papers + 3 WM-eval suites that all skip robomimic): **LDP — "Latent Diffusion Planning"** (2504.16925) *does* report robomimic image Lift/Can/Square = **0.69 / 0.70 / 0.46** (low-data 64×64), the single closest published number-for-number comparison to us. A separate, sharper challenge to the planning premise itself: **MR.Q** (2606.05555, Obando-Ceron/Fujimoto/Bacon/Courville/Castro) argues *predictive representation learning, not model-based planning*, is the driver of scalable multitask continuous control — a model-free actor-critic with auxiliary predictive objectives **beats a world-model planner without any planning**. We engage this head-on: our intuition head *is* such a predictive representation, and our claim is the narrow one (planning helps only where the WM sees the task-critical variable, useless/harmful on contact).

**Note on Temporal Straightening (2603.12231, same Balestriero/LeCun/Rudner/Gaoyue-Zhou lineage as LeWM/PLDM).** This is the field attacking the *planning landscape* from the representation side: a curvature regularizer straightens latent trajectories so Euclidean cost $\approx$ geodesic, making gradient/sampling planning better-conditioned and lifting goal-reaching SR. It is a clean **corroboration-and-contrast** for us. Corroboration: it confirms that *latent-planning quality is rate-limited by the geometry of the learned latent*, not just the planner — consistent with our per-family story. Contrast (the sharp differentiation): straightening helps where the task-critical variable **is in the latent but poorly conditioned** (goal-reaching geometry); it **cannot** help our robomimic failures, for *neither* of their two causes is the landscape curvature it targets. On **Lift** the success variable (grasp of a small cube) is *under-exposed* in the single frame (probe AUC $0.82$ vs $0.99$ from short history) — a **perception** limit, with no clean axis to straighten. On **Can/Square** the variable *is* present in the latent (probe $0.96$/$0.999$) but the task is **multi-stage** and a flat goal-image cost cannot decompose approach$\to$grasp$\to$transport$\to$place — a **horizon/decomposition** limit (WorldDP's diagnosis, §4). So our contribution is **orthogonal**: temporal straightening conditions the *geometry* of goal-reaching, whereas the robomimic failures are *perceptual* (Lift) and *structural* (Can/Square), recovered by history-conditioned execution rather than by smoothing the metric. (⚠️ This corrects an earlier draft that over-claimed grasp is "absent from the single-frame latent" universally — true only on Lift; the multi-task probe, EXPERIMENTS.md §6, shows Can/Square single-frame already decode grasp at $0.96$/$0.999$.)

**Note on NextLat (2511.05963, Microsoft Research — Teoh/Tomar/Ahn/Hu/Pearce/Sharma/Krishnamurthy/Islam/Lamb/Langford).** The closest sibling on the *objective* axis, from a different domain (sequence/LLM transformers, not pixel control). NextLat augments next-*token* training with a self-supervised **next-latent prediction** auxiliary — the model predicts its own future latent, the same training signal as our JEPA next-embedding loss — and shows this induces a **compact belief state** that improves world-modeling, reasoning, planning, and language, with 3.3× faster self-speculative decoding as a side effect. Two bearings on us. (i) **Independent corroboration of the objective:** at LLM scale, *predicting the next latent* (rather than reconstructing observations or next tokens alone) is what yields the compact, plannable representation — the principle our whole base WM rests on, now validated outside vision/control. (ii) **Belief-state framing supports our history finding:** their compact belief state summarizes history into a sufficient latent, the LLM-domain analogue of our result that a *history-conditioned* latent recovers the contact state a single frame cannot (`P(succ|grasp)` 76.2→99.3, §3). **Orthogonal to our DINOv2-init ablation** — they vary the training *objective*, we vary the encoder *weights*; the two axes compose. **Contrast (the caveat):** NextLat reports broad planning gains from the better latent, whereas our per-family rule is the limit — a better-objective latent still does not let flat-cost CEM solve long-horizon contact (Can/Square), which needs subgoal decomposition (§4, WorldDP). Pre-cutoff (Nov 2025); fields verified against the PDF via web.

**Note on SMWM — Sensorimotor World Models (2606.20104, Ivashkov/Balestriero/Schölkopf, Jun 2026) [post-cutoff]. ⚠️ THE CLOSEST SIBLING + a scoop-risk on our SIGReg-redundancy axis — same Balestriero as LeWM.** SMWM is a LeWM-style latent JEPA world model trained **end-to-end with an inverse-dynamics regularizer**: a learnable IDM predicts the action from consecutive latents, and *this single signal* both **prevents representation collapse** and **aligns the latent to the controllable degrees of freedom** (discarding uncontrollable distractors) — explicitly **without frozen encoders, EMA targets, or a variance/covariance latent regularizer (SIGReg/VICReg)**. Trained from offline reward-free trajectories; **planning-only — no learned action policy**, evaluated on 2D nav + 3D control from pixels. **The sharp implication for us:** SMWM's central thesis *is* our "predict-the-action-directly ⇒ SIGReg becomes redundant" conjecture (the IDM/action signal anchors the latent), now **published, by the LeWM author**. So that mechanism is **prior art — do NOT claim it as a LeWAM contribution**; our `sigreg_off` ablation should be framed as a **confirmation of / consistent-with SMWM**, citing it, not as a novel finding. **The clean differentiation that survives:** SMWM uses inverse dynamics as the WM's *anti-collapse regularizer for planning* (no policy, still searches at test); **LeWAM keeps the LeWM base and adds a *planning-free action head*** (one forward pass to BC / goal-conditioned control) — the efficiency-Pareto ($\sim$1104× fewer FLOPs than CEM) and the three-settings unification. So LeWAM's contribution is the **planning-free control + efficiency + per-family analysis**, explicitly **not** the IDM-as-anti-collapse, which SMWM owns. ⚠️ Verify against the PDF before citing exact task names/numbers (Tables not fully extracted).

## 2. Learned policy as a planning prior — the mechanism is owned; two specific gaps remain

Four axes per method. **A1 conditioning:** instantaneous `π(a|z_t)` vs history; if history, state-only vs **also past-action** (`a_{<t}`). **A2 output:** raw action / chunk / latent. **A3 native:** prior inside a joint-embedding/decoder-free latent WM? **A4 role:** warm-start search / replace search / actor-critic.

| TD-MPC (2203.04955) | Markovian | raw action | yes | warm-start MPPI |
| TD-M(PC)² (2502.03550) | Markovian (+policy constraint) | raw action | yes | warm-start MPPI |

**Verdict A1 — does anyone condition the planning prior on PAST ACTIONS?** Almost no one. Every warm-start prior is Markovian on the current (latent) state. The only precedent is **MBOP** (`a_t = f_b(s_t, a_{t-1})`), and it is weaker on every count: single previous action, feed-forward (not autoregressive over `a_{<t}`), no state history, raw-action target, non-JEPA. DINO-WM / V-JEPA 2-AC condition their *dynamics* on `(z,a)` history but carry **no prior at all**. → **An autoregressive state-and-action-history prior on a JEPA WM that warm-starts search is unoccupied.**

**Verdict A2 — does anyone warm-start search with a LATENT/embedding intuition (not raw actions)?** No. All warm-starters output raw actions (incl. JEPA-native PiJEPA). Embedding-space methods either **replace** search (GC-IDM, Diffuser, Dreamer) or are **pure policies** (ACT-JEPA, no planner). → **An embedding-space, action-embedding-target intuition whose job is to warm-start CEM is unclaimed**; the "helps even where raw decode is at the data floor" framing is novel.

**Framing lineage to cite (non-negotiable):** **ExIt / "Thinking Fast and Slow with DL and Tree Search"** (1705.08439, 2017) owns the "intuition" / System-1–System-2 / policy-guides-search vocabulary. Use their term, cite them.

## 3. Why pure CEM fails on contact-rich manipulation — the crux of the positioning

CEM-planning wins only when **(a)** the cost discriminates task progress **and (b)** the WM is accurate for action selection. Contact manipulation breaks both:
1. **The goal-image-latent cost is flat around the grasp.** The goal image is the end state ("object lifted"); latent distance to it cannot rank "about to grasp" vs "missed." The discriminative contact event is not in the cost.
2. **The WM does not model contact dynamics.** A latent JEPA trained for pixel-latent consistency predicts rigid contact / gripper interaction unreliably — exactly where CEM must roll out candidates.
3. **Contact state ("am I holding it") is invisible in pixels.** It is in neither the cost nor the rollout, so CEM drives the arm to the right *pose* (what it can represent) but cannot grasp → success ≈ 0.

**Field corroboration:** V-JEPA 2-AC's real-arm **grasp 25–65%** under pure CEM (2506.09985). **Our evidence:** robomimic planning ≈ 0; putting the WM in the action-selection loop *drops* Lift to ~30 vs **history-aware BC 96.7**; the mechanism is contact-state memory (action-history BC raises post-grasp `P(success|grasp)` **76.2 → 99.3**, grasp attainment unchanged — `project_gip_intention_format_eval_convention`).

**Honest caveat (must resolve before claiming).** Part of our specific robomimic "0%" was a **config artifact**: a *mid-demo goal image* with a *decoupled native robosuite success* — planning to a mid-demo image never triggers native task-success. **Cube with an aligned goal/success (`set_target_pos`, success = block within 0.04 m) gives 78% pure planning** (`project_cube_reproduction`), so CEM is **not** fundamentally 0 on manipulation. **Required experiment:** re-run robomimic CEM with the **final-demo (success-state) frame as the goal** (Cube-style alignment) and native success. Runnable now — `eval_gip_robomimic.py` (thin wrapper: registers `swm/RoboMimic-v0`, runs `eval_gip.py` with `gip_eval.mode = bc|guided|planning`); the goal alignment is the config to fix (`goal_offset_steps` → demo end). Two outcomes, both useful: aligned CEM jumps → the old 0 was mostly the artifact, reposition; aligned CEM still ≈0 → contact-state argument is clean and strong.
